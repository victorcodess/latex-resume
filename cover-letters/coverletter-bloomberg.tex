\noindent Dear \hiringmanager,

Some of the most rewarding moments in my work as an engineer have come from taking a problem that initially feels unfamiliar, working through its complexities, and eventually seeing the solution become something people can use. That process is what drew me to software engineering, and it's what excites me about the opportunity to join Bloomberg as a Software Engineer in Dublin.

I graduated from Covenant University with a B.Eng. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, where I developed a foundation in analytical problem-solving, data structures and algorithms, and artificial intelligence. Since then, I've worked with more than a dozen startups, building and shipping software used in real products. At SELECT, I designed CMS infrastructure with Sanity and Next.js, automated the migration of over 100 articles and documentation pages, and built a search experience that returns results in under a second. At Mono, I developed a frontend for labeling more than 490,000 financial transactions and integrated 16 REST API endpoints. These experiences taught me to take ownership of my work, think carefully about how systems fit together, and pay attention to the details that make software dependable.

Outside work, I've continued exploring problems that interest me. I built Orin, a voice-enabled AI companion with real-time conversations, authentication, and usage controls, and Nexus UI, an open-source React component library for AI applications with over 100 GitHub stars. Building these projects has pushed me to learn unfamiliar technologies, make architectural decisions, and take responsibility for the quality of what I ship. Leading Google Developer Group on Campus at Covenant University also gave me the opportunity to work with a team, mentor other students, and help create a community where people could learn from one another.

Bloomberg appeals to me because its engineers work on software that sits at the intersection of technology, data, and the financial markets, with real demands for thoughtful problem-solving and reliable execution. I'd value the opportunity to learn from experienced engineers, take on challenging problems, and contribute to products that people around the world rely on. I would bring curiosity, initiative, and a genuine enjoyment of building software, along with the willingness to keep learning and take ownership of the results.
