I have always been drawn to the part of engineering where an idea has to become something real. I recently built Orin, a voice-enabled AI companion with real-time voice calls, authentication, usage controls, and a WebSocket architecture. I also built Nexus UI, an open-source React component library for AI applications that has grown to more than 100 GitHub stars. Working on these projects has pushed me to explore real-time systems, APIs, testing, developer tooling, and the practical challenges of building reliable software.

Morgan Stanley's Technology Full Time Analyst Programme in London appeals to me because of the scale and depth of its engineering challenges. I want to work alongside experienced engineers, strengthen my technical foundations, and contribute to systems where reliability, performance, data, and thoughtful engineering matter. I would bring curiosity, strong engineering fundamentals, and a habit of learning by doing. Most importantly, I would bring genuine enthusiasm for becoming the kind of engineer who can be trusted with difficult problems and who takes pride in building things that hold up when they matter.
