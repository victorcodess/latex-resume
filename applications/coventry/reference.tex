October 4, 2026

To Coventry University,

Dear Admissions Committee,

\begin{center}
\textbf{LETTER OF RECOMMENDATION: VICTOR WILLIAMS}
\end{center}

I am writing to recommend Victor Williams for admission to the MSc Advanced Software Engineering at Coventry University. I was his level adviser and lecturer in Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Covenant University. I watched him through the degree: in his courses, in the communities he led on campus, and in his final-year thesis.

Academically, Victor was diligent. He graduated in October 2025 with a B.Eng in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Second Class Upper, and a CGPA of 4.24 on a 5.0 scale. That result came from steady effort across the programme. He prepared for his courses, he followed the work through, and he did not rely on a late burst of revision. He showed particular effort and interest in the courses closest to software and artificial intelligence, including Data Structures and Algorithms and Artificial Intelligence and Applications. He wanted to understand the material, and he wanted to know how it could be used.

He was also a student leader, and he used that position to help other students learn technology. He led the Google Developer Group on Campus, growing the community to more than 800 members and directing a core team of 11 students in more than 50 workshops and events, including TechFest, the university's largest technology conference. He led the Microsoft Learn Student Ambassadors community on campus, and he led Hebron Startup Labs. In each of these, his aim was the same: to give students practical experience with technology and the confidence to build with it.

His final-year thesis demanded persistence. He designed and built a power quality measuring device for distributed networks. The work was difficult, and he stayed with it until the device was complete. He was awarded an A. I saw in that project the same diligence I had already seen in his coursework. When a problem resisted the first attempt, he continued.

During the degree he was also working. He took contract and freelance software roles with startups while he was still a full-time student, and he did so without letting his academic work drop. That combination told me something I had already begun to see in class: a genuine interest in software and technology, and the discipline to pursue it alongside a demanding engineering degree.

The MSc Advanced Software Engineering at Coventry University will suit a graduate who has already shown academic diligence, a real interest in software engineering, and the perseverance to finish hard technical work. Victor showed all three, and he spent his years as a student helping others learn. I recommend him for admission.

If you require any further information, please contact me at chinemere.owuama@covenantuniversity.edu.ng or +234 703 967 8434.

Thank you.

Sincerely,
