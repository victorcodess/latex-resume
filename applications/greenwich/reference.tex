To the University of Greenwich,

Dear Admissions Committee,

\begin{center}
\textbf{LETTER OF RECOMMENDATION: VICTOR WILLIAMS}
\end{center}

I am writing to recommend Victor Williams for the MSc Computer Science at the University of Greenwich. I was his level adviser and lecturer in Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Covenant University.

He graduated in October 2025 with a B.Eng in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Second Class Upper, and a CGPA of 4.24/5.0. He was diligent across his courses and showed particular interest in software and artificial intelligence, including Data Structures and Algorithms and Artificial Intelligence and Applications.

He also led other students. As Lead of the Google Developer Group on Campus, he grew the community to more than 800 members and directed a team of 11 in more than 50 workshops and events, including TechFest. He led the Microsoft Learn Student Ambassadors community and Hebron Startup Labs, so that students could gain practical experience with technology.

For his final-year thesis he designed and built a power quality measuring device for distributed networks. The work was difficult, and he stayed with it. He was awarded an A. During the degree he also took contract and freelance software roles with startups, without letting his academic work drop.

I recommend him for admission. He has shown academic diligence, a real interest in software and intelligent systems, and the perseverance to finish hard technical work.

If you need any further information, please contact me at chinemere.owuama@covenantuniversity.edu.ng or +234 703 967 8434.
