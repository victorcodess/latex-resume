My decision to study MSc Software Engineering at the University of East London is a natural progression from my engineering education and professional experience in software development. I graduated from Covenant University with a B.Eng in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, where I developed a strong foundation in problem-solving and studied Data Structures and Algorithms and Artificial Intelligence and Applications. I now want to deepen my software engineering knowledge and develop the advanced technical skills needed to design, test and deliver more complex systems.

At Mono, I developed a TypeScript and Preact application for labelling more than 490,000 financial transactions and integrated 16 REST API endpoints. At Kora, I built a React and TypeScript marketplace application and integrated Google Maps APIs for location-based discovery. At SELECT, I developed headless CMS infrastructure using Next.js and Sanity, automated the migration of more than 100 pieces of content using Node.js, and built a documentation search system using Algolia. Through freelance work, I have also delivered production software for multiple startups, strengthening my ability to work independently, understand requirements and build maintainable solutions.

My interest has expanded beyond application development through projects involving artificial intelligence. I built Orin, a voice-enabled AI companion using Next.js, Supabase, the Vercel AI SDK, ElevenLabs and Node WebSockets. I also created Nexus UI, an open-source React component library for AI applications, with support for model selection, streaming and citations, alongside a RAG-powered AI chat system. These projects have made me increasingly interested in the deeper principles behind intelligent systems, data processing, system architecture and computational performance.

I have chosen East London because the MSc offers the breadth and depth I am looking for. I am particularly interested in Advanced Software Engineering, Web and Mobile App Development, Cloud Computing, and Software Testing and Quality Assurance. The opportunity to study requirements, design, testing and full-stack development would expand my existing software engineering capabilities. I am also attracted to the dissertation, which would let me investigate an industry-relevant problem and develop my analytical and technical skills.

After completing the MSc, I intend to continue my career as a software engineer while moving towards roles involving artificial intelligence, distributed systems and advanced computing. In the longer term, I want to take greater responsibility for system architecture and technical decision-making and build software that addresses meaningful problems. I also hope to contribute to the technology ecosystem in Nigeria and internationally by applying advanced computing knowledge to products with practical value.
