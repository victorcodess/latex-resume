My interest in Artificial Intelligence has developed from my background in engineering and my experience as a software engineer. I graduated from Covenant University with a B.Eng in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, where I studied Data Structures and Algorithms and Artificial Intelligence and Applications. Since then, I have built software professionally and independently, increasingly working with AI-enabled products. I now want to specialise in Artificial Intelligence and develop the theoretical and practical skills required to build reliable intelligent systems.

My undergraduate studies gave me a foundation in engineering problem-solving, programming and computational thinking. I have strengthened this through professional experience. At Mono, I developed a TypeScript and Preact application for labelling more than 490,000 financial transactions, supporting data used by models for credit scoring and loan default prediction. At Kora, I developed a React and TypeScript marketplace application and integrated Google Maps APIs for location-based discovery. At SELECT, I built headless CMS infrastructure with Next.js and Sanity, automated the migration of more than 100 pieces of content using Node.js, and developed a documentation search system using Algolia. Through freelance work, I have also delivered production software for multiple startups, giving me experience working with real-world requirements, APIs, data and different technology stacks.

My interest in AI has grown particularly through my personal projects. I built Orin, a voice-enabled AI companion using Next.js, Supabase, the Vercel AI SDK, ElevenLabs and Node WebSockets. I also developed Nexus UI, an open-source component library for AI applications, supporting model selection, streaming and citations, alongside a RAG-powered AI chat system. These projects have made me interested not only in integrating existing AI models, but also in understanding the techniques behind intelligent systems and how they can be made reliable and useful.

I have chosen the MSc Artificial Intelligence at the University of Salford because its focus aligns closely with the areas I want to develop. I am particularly interested in machine learning and data mining, deep learning, natural language processing and big data. The opportunity to develop practical experience with technologies such as Python, TensorFlow, Keras, Apache Spark and Azure Machine Learning would complement my existing software engineering skills and give me a stronger foundation in AI. I am also attracted to the programme's practical and research-oriented approach and its connection with the wider AI ecosystem in Greater Manchester.

After completing the MSc, I intend to work as an AI or machine learning engineer, combining my software engineering experience with advanced knowledge of artificial intelligence. In the longer term, I want to take responsibility for designing intelligent systems and developing AI-powered products that solve meaningful problems. I also hope to contribute to the growing technology ecosystem in Nigeria and internationally.
